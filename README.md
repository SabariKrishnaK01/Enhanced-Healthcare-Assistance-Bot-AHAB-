Enhanced Healthcare Assistance Bot (AHAB)

Your Professional AI Health Companion

Overview

AHAB (Advanced Healthcare Assistance Bot) is an intelligent AI-powered healthcare assistant that provides preliminary symptom assessment, treatment guidance, and health information for common medical conditions.

 Important Disclaimer: AHAB provides health information and guidance only. It does NOT replace professional medical diagnosis or treatment. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical concerns.

 Features

- 🔍 **Symptom Assessment**: AI-powered analysis of user symptoms
- 💊 **Treatment Guidance**: Age-appropriate medication and home remedy recommendations
- 📚 **Health Encyclopedia**: Comprehensive information on common conditions
- 🎯 **Personalized Care**: Customized advice based on user profile
- 🚨 **Emergency Guidelines**: When to seek immediate medical attention
- 📊 **Health Tracking**: Session history and health summaries

 🏥 Supported Conditions

- Fever & Temperature Management
- Common Cold & Respiratory Issues
- Cough & Throat Conditions
- Headache & Migraine Management
